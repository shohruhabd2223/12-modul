Endpoint map

NovaMarket uchun kerakli endpointlar

GET/api/products/
Katalogdagi barcha product cardlar uchun.
GET/api/products/<id>/
“Ko‘rish” tugmasi bosilganda bitta product uchun.
QUERY?category=smartfon
Smartfon, Laptop, Aksessuar filterlari uchun.
QUERY?search=&ordering=&page=
Qidiruv, sort va pagination uchun.
Product model → ProductSerializer → ProductListAPIView → ProductDetailAPIView → urls.py
